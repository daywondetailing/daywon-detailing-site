Questions

Do you come to me?

Yes. We're a mobile service and come to your address anywhere within 15 miles of Silver Spring, MD.

Why don't you list prices?

Price depends on the size of your vehicle. Send a quote request and we'll reply with a price for your car before anything is booked. Add-on prices are listed on the services page.

How long does a detail take?
Will my carpets or seats be wet afterwards?

With the Deep Restoration Detail, possibly. Extraction can leave carpets and cloth seats slightly damp after the appointment. Drying time depends on temperature, humidity, vehicle ventilation, and the amount of extraction needed.

Does the Full Interior + Exterior Detail include wax or tire shine?

No. Spray wax and tire shine are available as add-ons. The Deep Restoration Detail includes both.

Can I add extras?

Yes. Add spray wax, tire shine, carpet extraction, seat extraction, or carpet + seat extraction to most packages. The Deep Restoration Detail already includes extraction, tire shine and spray wax.

What if I'm not happy with the job?

Talk to us. If you're not happy with the work, we'll make it right.

When do I pay?

After the job is done. Nothing is charged online. We take payment through Square when we finish, including tap to pay on our phone.

How do I book?

Request a quote with your vehicle, address and preferred time. We reply within 1 to 2 hours with a price and confirm the appointment.
